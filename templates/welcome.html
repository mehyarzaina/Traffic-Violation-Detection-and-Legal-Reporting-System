How it works

Three steps from photo to record

  1. {{ icon('camera', 22) }}

    Send a photo

    Take or choose a photo of a possible violation. Your location is attached so reviewers know where it happened.

  2. {{ icon('shield-check', 22) }}

    AI and a reviewer check it

    Computer vision flags the violation and reads the vehicle details. An authorised reviewer then confirms or rejects it.

  3. {{ icon('file', 22) }}

    Confirmed reports are recorded

    Only reports a reviewer confirms become violation records, used for statistics and hotspot maps.

What you can report

Two violation types, detected automatically

{{ icon('no-entry', 22) }}

Wrong-way driving

Vehicles travelling against the designated direction of traffic, one of the most dangerous behaviours on the road.

{{ icon('parking', 22) }}

Illegal parking

Vehicles parked where they block traffic, pavements, junctions or no-parking zones.

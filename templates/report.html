How reporting works

  1. Take or choose a photo of a car driving the wrong way or parked illegally.
  2. Send it. We attach the place where you are, so the reviewer knows where it happened.
  3. A reviewer checks it. Only authorised reviewers see the result. You will see your report in My reports.

Stay safe and fair

  • Never use your phone while you are driving.
  • Only photograph public roads. Avoid people's faces where you can.
  • Don't send false or malicious reports.
  • This is a university prototype, not an official police service. In an emergency call 911.

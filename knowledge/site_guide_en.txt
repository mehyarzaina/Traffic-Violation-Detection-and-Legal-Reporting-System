ID: SITE-001
CATEGORY: Website — About
TITLE: What SmartPath Jordan is
SOURCE: SmartPath Jordan website
TEXT:
SmartPath Jordan is a university capstone prototype built at Al Hussein Technical University (HTU), Amman. It lets members of the public send photos of possible traffic violations, and lets authorised reviewers (officers) check them.

It is not an official Public Security Directorate (PSD) service and does not issue real fines. In an emergency call 911.

Two violation types are detected by the AI models: wrong-way driving and wrong (illegal) parking. The AI only suggests a result; a human reviewer always decides.

ID: SITE-002
CATEGORY: Website — Reporting
TITLE: How to send a report
SOURCE: SmartPath Jordan website
TEXT:
1. Sign in (or create an account) and open "New report".
2. Choose or take a photo of the vehicle. Only photograph public roads, and never use your phone while driving.
3. Allow location access so reviewers know where it happened.
4. Tick the box confirming the photo rules, then press "Send report".
5. You will see "Report received". An authorised reviewer checks every report.

Reporters are not told the outcome of a report, and they do not see plate numbers, car details or fines. This protects the privacy of the people in the photos.

ID: SITE-003
CATEGORY: Website — Reporting
TITLE: My reports, withdrawing a report and account options
SOURCE: SmartPath Jordan website
TEXT:
"My reports" lists the photos you sent and when. "Remove report" deletes a report that has not been reviewed yet; reports that were already reviewed stay with the reviewers but disappear from your list.

On the Account page you can change your password, download your data (JSON) or delete your account.

ID: SITE-004
CATEGORY: Website — Officers
TITLE: How officers review citizen reports
SOURCE: SmartPath Jordan website
TEXT:
Officers open "Reports" to see the review queue. Each report shows the photo with the AI boxes, the AI result, plate, vehicle, place and reporter. The officer ticks the violation type(s) and presses "Confirm violation", or presses "Not a violation". Only confirmed reports become official violation records (Records, Analytics, statistics). Decisions can be changed later.

Photos uploaded by an officer on the Upload page are recorded immediately.

ID: SITE-005
CATEGORY: Website — Fines
TITLE: Reference fines shown on the website
SOURCE: SmartPath Jordan website
TEXT:
The fines stored in the website (wrong-way driving 250 JD, wrong parking 30 JD) are demo reference values used for statistics and PDF reports. They are not official penalties. The legal penalty depends on the exact circumstances described in the traffic law (for example, whether the road is divided) and is decided by the authorities.

ID: SITE-006
CATEGORY: Website — Privacy
TITLE: Privacy, data kept and policies
SOURCE: SmartPath Jordan website
TEXT:
The site keeps only what it needs: the account details, the photo (location metadata is removed from the file), the GPS position you allow, and the review result. Photos are processed by an AI model on the server and may be sent to Google Gemini to read the plate; chat questions are sent to Groq. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap.

The Privacy Policy, Terms and Cookie Policy are linked in the footer, in English and Arabic. They were written with Jordan's Personal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023 in mind, but have not been reviewed by a lawyer, so the site does not claim legal compliance. The site only uses essential cookies (sign-in session) and a theme preference, so there are no tracking or advertising cookies.

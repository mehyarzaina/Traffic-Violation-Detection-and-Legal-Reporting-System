ARTICLE_ID: ART-001
CATEGORY: Definitions
TITLE: Law Title and Commencement
LEGAL_TEXT:
This law is called the "Traffic Law of 2008" (Law No. 49 of 2008). It came into effect thirty days after the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. It was subsequently amended by Law No. 18 of 2023, which came into force on 12 September 2023.

The law governs all aspects of road traffic in the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan, including vehicle registration and licensing, driver licensing, traffic violations, penalties, and road safety enforcement. It applies to all roads open to public traffic, including urban streets, highways, bridges, and tunnels.

PENALTY: Not applicable (definitional article)
KEYWORDS: traffic law, Jordan, 2008, 49, commencement, amendment, 2023, law no 18, road safety, PSD, Public Security Directorate

ARTICLE_ID: ART-002
CATEGORY: Definitions
TITLE: Key Definitions
LEGAL_TEXT:
The following definitions apply throughout the Traffic Law:

- Ministry: The Ministry of Interior.
- Minister: The Minister of Interior.
- Directorate: Public Security Directorate (PSD).
- Director: Director of Public Security.
- Licensing Department: The department responsible for licensing drivers and vehicles.
- Technical Office: The Central Technical Office for Traffic Affairs.
- Vehicle: Every means of land transport operating by mechanical force, including tractors, trailers, and semi-trailers. Does not include rail transport.
- Passenger Car: A vehicle designed to carry no more than 9 persons including the driver.
- Medium Bus: A vehicle designed to carry more than 9 and not more than 30 persons including the driver.
- Bus: A vehicle designed to carry more than 30 persons.
- Freight Vehicle: A vehicle designed to transport goods.
- Motorcycle: A two- or three-wheeled vehicle with an automatic engine designed to transport persons or goods.
- Driver: The person who drives the vehicle.
- Pedestrian: Any person on foot, including persons pushing strollers, wheelchairs, or handcarts.
- Passenger: Any person inside the vehicle except the driver.
- Traffic Accident: An incident caused by at least one moving vehicle resulting in damage to persons or property, or both. Intentionally caused incidents are not considered traffic accidents under this law.
- Traffic Violation: Infractions and misdemeanours set forth in this law.
- Safe Following Distance: The distance that must be maintained between a vehicle and the vehicle ahead during movement.
- Overtaking: Passing any vehicle or obstacle on the road.
- Road: Any way devoted to public traffic, including vehicular and pedestrian traffic, and includes bridges, tunnels, and parking areas.
- Highway (Limited Access): A route that does not allow entry or exit except from specific designated locations.
- Signal Path: Traffic lights, pillars, lines, phrases, words, or symbols on or above the road used to regulate, warn, or guide road users.

PENALTY: Not applicable (definitional article)
KEYWORDS: definitions, vehicle, driver, pedestrian, motorcycle, bus, freight, road, highway, overtaking, traffic accident, traffic violation, passenger, medium bus

ARTICLE_ID: ART-003
CATEGORY: Vehicle Registration and Licensing
TITLE: Vehicle Registration Requirements
LEGAL_TEXT:
All vehicles — including those belonging to ministries, departments, official and public institutions, municipalities, and joint services councils — must be registered and licensed in the Licensing Department.

No vehicle may be used in the Kingdom unless it is registered, licensed, and carries the prescribed number plates.

Exemptions apply to:
1. Vehicles belonging to the Armed Forces, Public Security, Civil Defence, Gendarmerie, and General Intelligence.
2. Foreign-licensed vehicles authorized to enter the Kingdom legally.
3. Trailers weighing less than 750 kg carried by farm tractors or designed for recreational, fishing, or tourism purposes.
4. Vehicles belonging to commercial agents, vehicle manufacturers, or research and development centers.

PENALTY: Impoundment of vehicle (see Article 24). Operating an unregistered vehicle is a primary basis for seizure.
KEYWORDS: registration, licensing, number plates, vehicle registration, unregistered vehicle, exemption, plates, kingdom of Jordan

ARTICLE_ID: ART-004
CATEGORY: Vehicle Registration and Licensing
TITLE: Vehicle Insurance Requirements
LEGAL_TEXT:
No vehicle may be registered, licensed, or have its license renewed without providing a valid insurance contract covering the full duration of the license period, issued by an insurance company licensed in the Kingdom.

The insurance must cover civil liability for damage to third parties resulting from the use of the vehicle, in accordance with the law governing insurance operations.

For tractor-trailer combinations, the tractor's insurance covers civil liability for damage caused by use of the attached trailer or semi-trailer.

No vehicle may enter Jordan unless it is covered by a valid civil liability insurance contract for the duration of its stay. Foreign vehicles must also carry a valid license from their country of origin for the duration of their stay in the Kingdom.

PENALTY: Fine of 30 JOD for driving a vehicle with an expired or non-existent insurance contract (Article 36, Item 29 — as amended 2023). License confiscation applies.
KEYWORDS: insurance, civil liability, third party, vehicle insurance, registration renewal, foreign vehicle, insurance contract, expired insurance

ARTICLE_ID: ART-005
CATEGORY: Vehicle Registration and Licensing
TITLE: Vehicle Number Plates
LEGAL_TEXT:
Each vehicle is assigned a unique number that distinguishes it from others upon registration and licensing. This number shall not be changed or altered except by the Licensing Department.

The regulations govern vehicle plates, including their exchange, printing, specifications, measurements, costs, and exemption cases.

It is a violation to:
- Drive a vehicle without front and rear number plates.
- Use forged, illegal, or non-standard number plates.
- Place any materials, additions, decorations, or reflectors on the number plates.
- Install additional number plates of any kind, shape, or content on the vehicle, or install more than one plate on top of another.
- Install number plates in violation of specifications or install a plate in a place other than its designated location.
- Manufacture vehicle number plates outside the Licensing Department.

PENALTY:
- Driving without number plates (front and rear): Fine of 200-300 JOD or imprisonment 1-2 months, or both (2023 amendment).
- Forged or illegal plates: Arrest without warrant, vehicle seizure (Article 25).
- Placing materials/decorations on plates: Fine of 50-100 JOD (Article 31, Item 14).
- Additional or non-standard plates: Fine of 30 JOD (Article 36, Items 21-22).
- Manufacturing plates outside Licensing Dept: Fine of 250-500 JOD or imprisonment 1-3 months (2023 amendment).
KEYWORDS: number plates, vehicle plates, forged plates, illegal plates, plate specifications, plate manufacture, decorations on plates, plate violations

ARTICLE_ID: ART-006
CATEGORY: Driver Licensing
TITLE: Driver Training and Licensing Requirements
LEGAL_TEXT:
No person may drive any vehicle on a public road unless they hold a valid driving license that entitles them to drive the category of that vehicle. The driver must carry the license while driving at all times.

Training to drive a vehicle may only be conducted at licensed driving training centers. Trainees must be at least 18 years of age.

No vehicle owner or holder may allow a person to drive who does not hold a valid driving license appropriate for that vehicle category.

A trainer (driving instructor) must be specifically authorized to conduct driver training by the Licensing Department.

Foreign nationals and Jordanians residing abroad may drive private passenger cars and rental/tourism vehicles using a valid foreign or international driving license, provided they are at least 18 years of age.

The Minister may suspend, confiscate, or cancel a driving license if the holder no longer meets the conditions required to hold it.

PENALTY:
- Driving without a driving license: Fine of 250-500 JOD or imprisonment 1-3 months, or both (2023 amendment).
- Allowing an unlicensed person to drive: Fine of 50-100 JOD (Article 31, Item 6).
- Training without a permit: Fine of 50-100 JOD (Article 31, Item 3 / 2023 amendment).
- Training a person under legal age: Fine of 50-100 JOD (Article 31, Item 20).
KEYWORDS: driving license, driver license, license category, unlicensed driving, driver training, training permit, foreign driving license, international license, 18 years, minimum age

ARTICLE_ID: ART-007
CATEGORY: Driver Licensing
TITLE: Driving License Points System
LEGAL_TEXT:
The Traffic Law establishes a points system for driver licenses. Points are accumulated based on the seriousness of the committed offence.

License suspension thresholds:
- 10 to fewer than 16 points: Driver must attend a rehabilitation course at the Traffic Institute. Attendance is optional for those below 10 points. Attending the course removes 6 points from the driver's record. This right may be used once per year.
- 16 to fewer than 20 points: License suspended for 2 months.
- 20 to fewer than 24 points: License suspended for 3 months.
- 24 to fewer than 28 points: License suspended for 4 months.
- 28 to fewer than 32 points: License suspended for 6 months.
- 32 points or more: License suspended for 1 year.

All points are written off when the suspension period is fully served. No monetary fines are imposed solely as a result of the points system.

PENALTY: Administrative license suspension as detailed above. No additional fines for the points system itself.
KEYWORDS: points system, driver points, license suspension, rehabilitation course, traffic institute, 16 points, 20 points, 24 points, 32 points, license withdrawal, administrative suspension

ARTICLE_ID: ART-008
CATEGORY: Traffic Violations — Serious Offences
TITLE: Grounds for Arrest Without Warrant
LEGAL_TEXT:
Any member of the Public Security may arrest without a warrant the driver of any vehicle and seize the driving license, vehicle license, driving permit, and training permit if the driver commits any of the following violations:

A. Causing the death of a person or injury to a person due to driving a vehicle.
B. Fleeing from the scene of a traffic accident the driver committed.
C. Driving a vehicle while not licensed to drive.
D. Driving a vehicle during the period of suspension of the driving license (administrative or judicial).
E. Driving a vehicle with a forged driving license, forged vehicle license, or forged permit.
F. Driving a vehicle with forged number plates or illegal number plates.
G. Driving a vehicle recklessly or showily (exhibition driving) on the road.
H. Driving a vehicle under the influence of alcohol or any psychotropic substances that cause the driver to lose control, consuming alcohol while driving, or refusing to take the legally prescribed examination for alcohol/narcotics.
I. Driving a stolen vehicle or one required to be seized for criminal proceedings.

PENALTY: Immediate arrest without warrant. All licenses and documents seized. Criminal prosecution follows under applicable articles (Articles 26, 27, 29).
KEYWORDS: arrest without warrant, reckless driving, drunk driving, alcohol, forged license, suspended license, fleeing accident, unlicensed driving, stolen vehicle, forged plates, exhibition driving

ARTICLE_ID: ART-009
CATEGORY: Traffic Violations — Criminal Offences
TITLE: First-Category Offences (Most Serious Violations)
LEGAL_TEXT:
The following constitute first-category (most serious) traffic offences, punishable by imprisonment and/or substantial fines:

GROUP A — Imprisonment 3 months to 6 months, OR fine 500-1,000 JOD, OR both:
1. A repair shop owner who repaired a vehicle involved in a traffic accident without informing the competent authorities.
2. Illegally harbouring any vehicle or part thereof with intent to sell or dispose of it without the owner's permission.
3. Fabricating a traffic accident (proven by judicial ruling).
4. Agreeing to replace the driver who caused a traffic accident resulting in human injury or death.
5. The driver fleeing the scene of an accident causing human injury or death, or failure to notify the nearest security station or patrol.
6. Driving vehicles prohibited from road use (military-exclusive vehicles, etc.) on public roads.

Note: All fines in Group A are doubled if the same violation is repeated within one year.

GROUP B — Imprisonment 1 month to 3 months, OR fine 250-500 JOD, OR both:
1. Driving under the influence of alcoholic beverages at a rate exceeding the permissible blood alcohol concentration limit (0.04% / 40 mg per 100 ml of blood). Commercial and novice drivers (fewer than 2 years' experience): zero tolerance.
2. The driver consuming alcoholic beverages while driving.
3. A driving instructor consuming alcoholic beverages during training.
4. Driving a vehicle carrying hazardous, explosive, or flammable materials inside populated areas or restricted zones without a permit, or leaving such a vehicle inside them.
5. Conducting a road race without prior authorization from the competent authorities.
6. Driving a vehicle without a driving license.
7. Driving a vehicle that spills or leaks oils, petroleum derivatives, or any hazardous materials on the road.
8. Driving a vehicle that requires a Class 1, 2, or 7 licence but doing so with a different category licence.
9. Driving under the influence of drugs or psychotropic substances.
10. Driving in the opposite direction on roads with separated directions (wrong-way driving).
11. Driving at night without front or rear lights on an unlit road.
12. Vehicle load protruding from front or rear by more than 100 cm without a permit.
13. Increasing the height of the vehicle with its load to 420 cm without a permit.
14. Manufacturing vehicle number plates outside the Licensing Department.
15. Replacing the front or rear part of a vehicle without prior approval from the Licensing Department.
16. Manufacturing trailers or semi-trailers without prior approval or issuing false certificates of origin.
17. Tampering with vehicle number plates (scratching, inking, gluing, or any method that changes or obscures plate numbers).

Note: All fines in Group B are doubled if the same violation is repeated within one year.

PENALTY: See fine and imprisonment ranges above per group.
KEYWORDS: first category offence, drunk driving, alcohol, drugs, reckless driving, wrong way driving, road race, hazardous materials, forged plates, fleeing accident, human injury, death, imprisonment, fabricated accident, blood alcohol, BAC

ARTICLE_ID: ART-010
CATEGORY: Traffic Violations — Criminal Offences
TITLE: Serious Violations — License Misuse and Impersonation
LEGAL_TEXT:
The following violations carry imprisonment of 1-3 months or a fine of 150-300 JOD, or both:

1. Allowing another person to use one's driving license, driving permit, training permit, or detention notice.
2. Using another person's driving license, permit, or detention notice.
3. Illegally using vehicle number plates or a vehicle license.
4. Agreeing to replace the driver who caused a traffic accident resulting in material (property) damage.
5. Using lanes designated for public transport with vehicles not authorized to do so.

When a vehicle's plates or license is used illegally, the driving license shall be suspended for 2 months.
All fines are doubled if the violation is repeated within one year.

PENALTY: Fine 150-300 JOD or imprisonment 1-3 months, or both. 2-month license suspension for illegal plate/license use.
KEYWORDS: license misuse, impersonation, driving permit, false identity, driving license sharing, illegal plates, vehicle license misuse, license suspension, public transport lane

ARTICLE_ID: ART-011
CATEGORY: Traffic Violations — Criminal Offences
TITLE: Reckless and Dangerous Driving
LEGAL_TEXT:
The following violations carry imprisonment of 1-2 months or a fine of 200-300 JOD, or both:

1. Leaving a broken-down vehicle on a road lane without placing reflectors or warning lights to indicate it.
2. Vehicle load protruding beyond the width of the vehicle without a permit or violating permit conditions.
3. Driving a vehicle while the driving license is administratively or judicially suspended.
4. Driving a vehicle with a foreign or international driving license when the Jordanian driving license has been administratively or judicially suspended.
5. Using an unauthorized private vehicle to transport passengers for a fee.
6. Using a copy (photocopy/image) of a vehicle license, driver's license, driving permit, or training permit with the intent to mislead or defraud.
7. Driving a Jordanian public vehicle (bus, freight vehicle) with a foreign or international driving license.
8. Throwing or spilling vehicle loads on public roads (stones, dust, construction waste, wastewater, liquid materials, etc.).
9. Using a vehicle for purposes other than those for which it is licensed (e.g., transporting passengers in rental vehicles, or carrying waste in unlicensed vehicles).
10. Driving public passenger vehicles, student transport vehicles, or hazardous materials transport vehicles without the required permit, or in violation of permit conditions.
11. Installing or using a danger alert or multi-sound device on vehicles not authorized to do so.
12. Installing or using emergency vehicle lights on unauthorized vehicles.
13. Failing to secure containers with chains or locks designated for this purpose on freight vehicles.

All fines are doubled if the violation is repeated within one year.

PENALTY: Fine 100-200 JOD or imprisonment 1-2 months, or both.
KEYWORDS: broken down vehicle, lane obstruction, suspended license, public vehicle, foreign license, illegal transport, cargo spill, unauthorized use, emergency lights, freight container, hazardous materials permit, student transport

ARTICLE_ID: ART-012
CATEGORY: Traffic Violations — Speeding
TITLE: Speeding Violations and Penalties
LEGAL_TEXT:
Speed limits in Jordan are set by regulation and vary by road type. Violations are graded by how much the speed limit is exceeded:

TIER 1 — Exceeding speed limit by more than 50 km/h:
Punishment: Imprisonment 1-3 months OR fine of 100 JOD, OR both.
Fine is doubled if the violation is repeated within one year.

TIER 2 — Exceeding speed limit by more than 30 km/h and up to 50 km/h:
Punishment: Fine of 30 JOD.
(Under original 2008 law; 2023 amendment may apply escalation)

TIER 3 — Exceeding speed limit by more than 10 km/h and up to 30 km/h:
Punishment: Fine of 20 JOD.
(Under original 2008 law; 2023 amendment may apply escalation)

TIER 4 — Exceeding speed limit by more than 20 km/h and up to 40 km/h:
Results in driving license seizure pending payment.

General speed provisions:
- Drivers must reduce speed when passing through populated areas, approaching schools, pedestrian crosswalks, curves, or intersections.
- Driving at a speed less than the minimum speed limit set on roads controlled by signs: Fine of 15 JOD.
- Freight vehicles whose gross weight exceeds 12 tonnes are subject to restricted times and locations of travel.

PENALTY: See tiers above. License seizure applies in specific ranges. Doubled fines for repeat violations within one year.
KEYWORDS: speeding, speed limit, excessive speed, 50 km/h, 30 km/h, 10 km/h, speed violation, school zone, pedestrian crossing, minimum speed, freight vehicle speed, speed camera

ARTICLE_ID: ART-013
CATEGORY: Traffic Violations — Lane Discipline
TITLE: Lane Violations and Wrong-Way Driving
LEGAL_TEXT:
The following lane-related violations and their penalties apply under the Traffic Law (as amended 2023):

WRONG-WAY DRIVING:
Driving a vehicle in the opposite direction of traffic flow on roads where directions are separated by a central island or median, or in the wrong lane, is a serious criminal offence.

Penalty: Fine 250-500 JOD or imprisonment 1-3 months, or both (2023 amendment, Group B offence). This applies to driving against designated traffic flow on divided roads.

LANE VIOLATIONS (fine of 50-100 JOD):
1. The driver took the wrong lane (lane misuse).
2. Driving the vehicle in the opposite direction of traffic or in violation of traffic prohibition signs.
3. Sudden or abrupt change of lane by the driver.
4. Freight vehicles, buses, medium buses, construction vehicles, and agricultural vehicles failing to drive on the right lane of a multi-lane road.
5. The driver's non-compliance with the stop sign or stop line.

LANE VIOLATIONS (fine of 30 JOD):
6. Driving a vehicle outside the designated lanes on the road.
7. Double parking on roads.
8. Stopping on a lane in a way that obstructs traffic.

AVOIDANCE MANOEUVRES (infrastructure diagnostic indicator for SmartPath Jordan):
When multiple vehicles repeatedly make identical lateral lane deviations or swerves at the same GPS coordinates, this may indicate a road defect (pothole, poor lane markings, damaged signage) rather than driver negligence. SmartPath Jordan classifies such clusters as Infrastructure Alerts rather than pure driver violations.

PENALTY: See amounts above per violation type. Wrong-way driving: 250-500 JOD or imprisonment.

CATEGORY: Traffic Violations — Traffic Signals
TITLE: Traffic Signal and Traffic Light Violations
LEGAL_TEXT:
Running a Red Light (Traffic Signal Violation):
Bypassing a red traffic light (signal) is a serious violation.

Penalty under 2023 amendment:
Fine of 200-300 JOD, OR imprisonment 1-2 months, OR both.

- Fine of 20 JOD for failure to comply with mandatory signs (stop, yield, one-way).

- Non-compliance with STOP sign: Grounds for driving license seizure.

Driver's non-compliance with signals from Public Security personnel while on duty:
Fine of 30 JOD (Article 36, Item 19).

PENALTY:
- Red light: 200-300 JOD or imprisonment 1-2 months (2023). Doubled if repeated within 1 year.
- Mandatory signs: 20 JOD.
- Signals from officer: 30 JOD.

ARTICLE_ID: ART-015
CATEGORY: Traffic Violations — Overtaking
TITLE: Overtaking (Passing) Violations
LEGAL_TEXT:
Overtaking (passing another vehicle) is governed by specific rules under the Traffic Law:

Wrong overtaking (passing incorrectly in cases and places where overtaking is prohibited):
Penalty: Fine of 50-100 JOD (Article 34, Item A — 2023 amendment).
Additionally, driving license may be seized pending payment when wrong overtaking occurs.

Specific overtaking violations:
- The driver must reduce speed when overtaking buses and medium buses while passengers are descending from them: Fine of 30 JOD (Article 36, Item 3).
- Overtaking in places or situations where overtaking is prohibited: Grounds for license seizure.

General rule:
Drivers must not pass other vehicles except where it is safe and legally permitted. Overtaking must be done on the right or left as designated by road signs and markings.

PENALTY:
- Wrong overtaking: Fine 50-100 JOD. Driving license seized pending payment.
- Failure to slow when overtaking buses/medium buses boarding/alighting passengers: Fine 30 JOD.

It is illegal to use a handheld mobile phone while driving a vehicle in motion.

- Eating food or drinking while driving a public vehicle: Fine of 15 JOD.
- Using audio/visual recording devices inside the vehicle in a way that contravenes public morals or causes noise and inconvenience: Fine of 20 JOD.

ARTICLE_ID: ART-017
CATEGORY: Traffic Violations — Seatbelts and Child Safety
TITLE: Seatbelt and Child Safety Violations
LEGAL_TEXT:
It is mandatory for all drivers and front-seat passengers to wear seatbelts while the vehicle is in motion. As per the 2023 amendment, this obligation extends to all passengers in all seats.

Seatbelt Violation:
- Not using the seatbelt for front seat passengers: Fine of 20 JOD (Article 37, Item 38 — 2023 amendment). The original 2008 law set this at 15 JOD.
- The seatbelt violation is an infraction for which the license is not seized; an alert is issued.

Child Safety:
- Children under the age of 10 must use an appropriate child seat or booster seat.
- Children aged 10 and above must wear a seatbelt.
- Allowing a child under 10 years of age to sit in the front passenger seat without a child seat/booster seat: Fine applies (Article 38, Item 36.22 of original law).
- Children must not sit in the front seat if under age 10 unless in a properly fitted child restraint.

Motorcycle Helmet:
- Riding a motorcycle without wearing a helmet — for both the driver and passenger: Fine of 30 JOD (Article 36, Item 14).

PENALTY:
- No seatbelt (front seat): 20 JOD.
- No child seat for under-10s in front: Fine applies.
- No helmet on motorcycle: 30 JOD.

ARTICLE_ID: ART-018
CATEGORY: Traffic Violations — Drunk and Impaired Driving
TITLE: Driving Under the Influence of Alcohol or Drugs
LEGAL_TEXT:
Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs is one of the most serious offences in Jordan's Traffic Law.

Blood Alcohol Concentration (BAC) Limit:
- Standard drivers: 0.04% (40 mg alcohol per 100 ml of blood).
- Commercial drivers and novice drivers (fewer than 2 years of experience): Zero tolerance (0.00% BAC).

Alcohol-Related Violations and Penalties:
1. Driving under the influence of alcohol at a rate exceeding the permissible BAC limit:
   Imprisonment 1-3 months OR fine of 250-500 JOD, OR both.

2. Consuming alcoholic beverages while driving:
   Imprisonment 1-3 months OR fine of 250-500 JOD, OR both.

3. Driving under the influence of drugs or psychotropic substances:
   Imprisonment 1-3 months OR fine of 250-500 JOD, OR both.

4. Refusing to undergo the legally prescribed alcohol or narcotics test when requested by Public Security:
   Grounds for arrest without warrant (2023 amendment explicitly grants Public Security authority to use devices to test alcohol and narcotic levels).

5. A driving instructor consuming alcohol during training:
   Imprisonment 1-3 months OR fine of 250-500 JOD, OR both.

Enforcement:
Public Security personnel are authorized under the 2023 amendment to use specific devices to gauge levels of alcohol and narcotics in drivers. Arrest without warrant applies when the driver loses control of the vehicle due to influence, or refuses the test.

PENALTY: 250-500 JOD or imprisonment 1-3 months (alcohol/drugs DUI). Arrest without warrant. License seizure.
KEYWORDS: drunk driving, DUI, alcohol, drugs, BAC, blood alcohol, psychotropic, narcotics, impaired driving, 0.04, zero tolerance, commercial driver, novice driver, breath test, intoxicated

Pedestrian Obligations:
Pedestrians must use designated pedestrian crossings where available. They must not obstruct traffic flow on roads.

CATEGORY: Traffic Violations — Parking Violations
TITLE: Parking Violations
LEGAL_TEXT:
The following parking violations and their penalties apply:

1. Parking the vehicle on sidewalks or pedestrian paths:
   Fine of 20 JOD.

2. Parking large trucks, freight vehicles, or dump trucks in residential neighbourhoods:
   Fine applies. Driving license may be seized. Specifically:
   - Freight vehicles with gross weight exceeding 12 tonnes: Fine of 30 JOD for parking in residential areas.

   Fine of 30 JOD (2023 amendment).

6. Parking before or after pedestrian crossings at a distance of less than 10 metres:
   Fine of 20 JOD.

   Fine of 100-200 JOD or imprisonment 1-2 months, or both (2023 amendment — serious violation).

8. Parking the vehicle on the sides of roads for excursion purposes in prohibited places:
   Fine of 15 JOD.

9. Leaving the vehicle at the head of a curve:

ARTICLE_ID: ART-028
CATEGORY: Traffic Violations — Accident Reporting
TITLE: Traffic Accident Obligations and Hit-and-Run
LEGAL_TEXT:
Drivers involved in traffic accidents have specific legal obligations under the Traffic Law.

HIT-AND-RUN — Fleeing the scene:
1. The driver fleeing the scene of an accident causing human injury or death, or failing to notify the nearest security station or police patrol:
   Imprisonment 3-6 months OR fine 500-1,000 JOD, OR both (Group A first-category offence — 2023 amendment).
   Arrest without warrant applies.

2. The driver fleeing the scene of an accident causing material (property) damage only, or failing to notify authorities:
   Imprisonment 2 weeks to 1 month OR fine 50-100 JOD, OR both.

OBLIGATIONS AFTER AN ACCIDENT:
- The driver must remain at the scene until authorities arrive.
- The driver must notify the nearest police station or patrol.
- The driver of a vehicle involved in a traffic accident causing material damage, if the vehicle's condition allows it, must move the vehicle to a safe location.
   Failure to do so: Fine of 30 JOD.

RELATED VIOLATIONS:
- Agreeing to replace the actual driver who caused an accident resulting in human injury:
   Imprisonment 3-6 months OR fine 500-1,000 JOD.

- Agreeing to replace the actual driver who caused an accident resulting in material damage:
   Imprisonment 1-3 months OR fine 150-300 JOD.

- A repair shop owner repairing a vehicle damaged in a traffic accident without notifying competent authorities:
   Imprisonment 3-6 months OR fine 500-1,000 JOD.

- Fabricating a traffic accident (proven by court ruling):
   Imprisonment 3-6 months OR fine 500-1,000 JOD.

- Flat-rate disclosure fee: A driver causing a traffic accident is charged a flat fee of 5 JOD for the investigation at the scene. Exempted if the accident results in a fatality.

PENALTY: See detailed amounts above per situation.

TITLE: Road Conditions, Maintenance Indicators, and Infrastructure Alerts
LEGAL_TEXT:
This article relates to the relationship between traffic violations and road infrastructure conditions — a core component of SmartPath Jordan's dual mission.

ROAD SPILLS AND HAZARDS created by drivers:
1. Throwing or spilling vehicle loads (stones, dust, construction waste, wastewater, liquid materials, etc.) on public roads:

4. Not removing stones or similar objects used to secure a parked broken-down vehicle once no longer needed:
   Fine of 20 JOD.

INFRASTRUCTURE ALERT METHODOLOGY (SmartPath Jordan):
Under the system's analytical framework, the following driver behaviours — when repeated by multiple unique vehicles at identical GPS coordinates within a defined time window — are reclassified from individual driver violations to Infrastructure Alerts (Road Defect indicators):

- Repeated sudden swerving or abrupt lateral lane changes at the same location → Likely pothole, road surface damage, or obstruction.
- Repeated avoidance manoeuvres at the same intersection or road section → Likely poor signage, damaged road markings, or road design defect.
- Clusters of wrong-lane entries at the same junction → Likely inadequate lane markings or confusing road layout.

These infrastructure alerts are forwarded to the Greater Amman Municipality (GAM) and the Ministry of Public Works and Housing (MoPWH) for proactive maintenance action, consistent with Jordan's Road Asset Management System (RAMS) objectives.

LEGAL BASIS FOR MAINTENANCE OBLIGATION:
The Ministry of Public Works and Housing is responsible for maintaining the 9,000 km national road network. The Greater Amman Municipality is responsible for over 7,500 km of urban roads in Amman. Both agencies are obligated to maintain roads in safe condition. Failure to address identified road defects that contribute to accidents may expose the state to civil liability under Jordan's Civil Law.

PENALTY: See fine amounts above for driver-side violations.

ARTICLE_ID: PARK-001
CATEGORY: Wrong Parking — Definition
TITLE: What is Wrong Parking?
LEGAL_TEXT:
Wrong parking (also called illegal parking or parking violation) refers to stopping or leaving a vehicle in any location that is not designated for parking, or in a way that violates traffic law or municipal regulations.

Wrong parking is one of the most common traffic violations in Jordan, particularly in Amman. It causes traffic congestion, blocks pedestrian paths, obstructs emergency vehicle access, and contributes to road accidents.

Wrong parking is detected by:
1. Traffic police officers on patrol.
2. Municipal parking enforcement officers.
3. AI-powered camera systems such as SmartPath Jordan.

When SmartPath Jordan detects a vehicle parked in a prohibited location based on video footage analysis, it generates an automatic violation report and submits it for officer review.

PENALTY: Fines range from 15 JOD to 30 JOD depending on the specific parking violation. Vehicle may be towed or wheel-clamped.

ARTICLE_ID: PARK-002
CATEGORY: Wrong Parking — Specific Violations and Fines
TITLE: All Wrong Parking Violations and Their Penalties
LEGAL_TEXT:
The following wrong parking violations and their fines are defined under Jordan Traffic Law No. 49 of 2008 as amended by Law No. 18 of 2023:

FINE OF 30 JOD:
1. Double parking — parking directly beside another parked vehicle, blocking it in.
2. Stopping or parking on a road lane in a way that obstructs traffic flow.
3. Parking at the entrances to public or private parking lots, blocking access.
4. Parking large freight vehicles (gross weight exceeding 12 tonnes) in residential neighbourhoods.

FINE OF 20 JOD:
5. Parking the vehicle on sidewalks or pedestrian paths — preventing pedestrian movement.
6. Parking within 20 metres before or after a road intersection or junction.
7. Parking within 10 metres before or after a pedestrian crossing (zebra crossing).
8. Parking the vehicle at the head of a curve — blocking visibility for oncoming vehicles.
9. Leaving a vehicle at road intersections and junctions at a distance less than 20 metres.
10. Leaving the vehicle before or after signal-controlled intersections in a way that obstructs traffic.
11. Failure to ensure vehicle stability while parked (not applying the handbrake).
12. Parking the vehicle at accident sites without justification.

FINE OF 15 JOD:
13. Parking public passenger vehicles for loading or unloading in places other than those designated for that purpose.
14. Parking the vehicle on the sides of roads for excursion purposes in prohibited places.
15. The vehicle stopped inside a roundabout without justification.
16. Parking in spaces designated for vehicles of persons with disabilities (without permit).

FINE OF 40 JOD:
17. Parking vehicles on bridges or inside tunnels without justification.

SERIOUS VIOLATION (Fine 100–200 JOD or imprisonment 1–2 months):
18. Leaving a broken-down vehicle on a road lane without placing reflectors or warning lights to indicate it to other drivers.

ADDITIONAL CONSEQUENCES:
- Vehicle may be towed (impounded) if parked in a way that causes serious obstruction.

ARTICLE_ID: WWD-001
CATEGORY: Wrong-Way Driving — Definition
TITLE: What is Wrong-Way Driving?
LEGAL_TEXT:
Wrong-way driving (also called driving in the opposite direction or driving against traffic) refers to operating a vehicle in a direction that is opposite to the designated flow of traffic on a road.

In Jordan, wrong-way driving is classified into two levels of severity:

LEVEL 1 — SERIOUS CRIMINAL OFFENCE:
Driving in the opposite direction on roads where traffic directions are separated by a central island, median, or physical barrier.
This is a Group B first-category offence under the 2023 amended Traffic Law.
Penalty: Fine 250–500 JOD OR imprisonment 1–3 months OR both.

LEVEL 2 — MODERATE VIOLATION:
Driving in the opposite direction of traffic or violating traffic prohibition signs on roads without a physical separator.
Penalty: Fine 50–100 JOD.

Wrong-way driving is one of the leading causes of fatal accidents in Jordan. According to Jordan's Traffic Department, wrong lane usage caused 29.3% of road fatalities in 2024. It is the second most common driver error in Jordan, accounting for 22.5% of all violations, yet it is rarely caught by existing threshold-based cameras.

SmartPath Jordan specifically targets wrong-way driving detection using YOLOv11 object detection and ByteTrack trajectory analysis to identify vehicles moving against the designated traffic flow.

PENALTY: 250–500 JOD or imprisonment 1–3 months (divided road). 50–100 JOD (undivided road).

TITLE: Legal Basis for Wrong-Way Driving Violations

Wrong-way driving is addressed in multiple articles of Jordan Traffic Law No. 49 of 2008 as amended by Law No. 18 of 2023:

Driving a vehicle in the opposite direction on roads with separated directions (roads divided by a central island or median barrier).
Penalty: Imprisonment 1–3 months OR fine 250–500 JOD OR both.
Note: Fine is doubled if the violation is repeated within one year.
Grounds for arrest without warrant if the driver loses control.

ARTICLE 34 (ITEM G — MODERATE):
Driving the vehicle in the opposite direction of traffic or violating signs prohibiting traffic in that direction.
Penalty: Fine 50–100 JOD.

ARTICLE 29 (ITEM D — ORIGINAL 2008 LAW):
Driving the vehicle in the opposite direction on roads separated by an island or median.
Penalty: Fine 100–200 JOD or imprisonment 1 week–1 month (original 2008 penalty before 2023 amendment increased it).

If wrong-way driving results in loss of vehicle control, causes injury or death, or the driver is found to be driving recklessly — arrest without warrant applies and all licenses are seized.

Driving the vehicle in the opposite direction on roads separated by a central island is grounds for vehicle impoundment for 24 hours to 30 days.

ARTICLE_ID: WWD-003
CATEGORY: Wrong-Way Driving — How SmartPath Jordan Detects It
TITLE: SmartPath Jordan Detection Methodology for Wrong-Way Driving
LEGAL_TEXT:
SmartPath Jordan detects wrong-way driving using a computer vision pipeline:

DETECTION PIPELINE:
1. YOLOv11 (object detection model) identifies all vehicles in each video frame.
2. ByteTrack (multi-object tracking algorithm) assigns a unique ID to each vehicle and tracks its movement across frames.
3. Trajectory analysis examines the direction of each vehicle's movement relative to the designated traffic flow direction for that road.
4. If a vehicle's trajectory is consistently opposite to the designated flow direction, it is classified as a wrong-way driving violation.
5. PaddleOCR reads the license plate number of the offending vehicle.
6. The RAG system retrieves the relevant legal article (WWD-002) and generates a violation report.
7. The report is submitted to a traffic officer for review via the SmartPath Jordan dashboard.

INFRASTRUCTURE ALERT DISTINCTION:
If multiple unique vehicles (5 or more within a 10-minute window) all deviate from their lane in the same direction at the same GPS coordinates — this is NOT classified as wrong-way driving by multiple drivers. Instead, it is classified as an Infrastructure Alert, indicating a likely road defect, missing lane markings, or confusing road layout. This alert is forwarded to the Greater Amman Municipality (GAM) for maintenance action.

CONFIDENCE THRESHOLD:
The system only flags a violation when the trajectory confidence score exceeds the defined threshold. Low-confidence detections are discarded to avoid false positives.

PENALTY: 250–500 JOD or imprisonment 1–3 months (if on divided road). Report generated automatically and submitted for officer review.

ARTICLE_ID: FAQ-004
CATEGORY: Infrastructure Alerts
TITLE: What is an Infrastructure Alert and What Should an Officer Do?
LEGAL_TEXT:
An Infrastructure Alert is generated by SmartPath Jordan when the system detects that multiple vehicles — not just one — are performing the same abnormal traffic manoeuvre at the same GPS location within a short time window.

TRIGGER CONDITION:
5 or more unique vehicle IDs exhibit identical lateral swerves, lane deviations, or avoidance manoeuvres at the same GPS coordinates within a 10-minute window.

WHAT IT MEANS:
When many different drivers make the same unusual manoeuvre at the same spot, the likely cause is NOT driver negligence — it is a physical road problem such as:
- A pothole or road surface damage.
- Missing or faded lane markings.
- Damaged or missing road signs.
- Poor road design at that junction.
- An obstacle or debris on the road.

WHAT HAPPENS:
1. SmartPath Jordan classifies the event as an Infrastructure Alert instead of a traffic violation.
2. Individual drivers are NOT fined — they are not at fault.
3. The alert is sent to the Municipal Engineer Dashboard showing the location on a map as a Red Zone.
4. The Greater Amman Municipality (GAM) or Ministry of Public Works and Housing (MoPWH) is notified to inspect and repair the location.

WHAT THE OFFICER SHOULD DO:
- If you see an Infrastructure Alert in your dashboard, do not issue fines to the individual drivers.
- Forward the alert to the relevant municipal authority.
- If the road defect is causing immediate danger, contact traffic management to place warning signs or close the lane temporarily.

PENALTY: No fines issued for Infrastructure Alerts. Maintenance action by GAM or MoPWH.

A: Yes. A vehicle may be towed if it causes serious obstruction to traffic flow or creates a safety hazard. The owner must pay the fine plus towing and storage fees to retrieve it.

Q: What law article covers wrong parking?
A: Article 37 (Items 3, 8, 9, 10, 11) and Article 36 (Items 2, 31) and Article 35 (Item 9) of the Traffic Law cover various wrong parking violations.

Q: Is wrong parking a criminal offence?
A: No. Wrong parking is a minor infraction (fine only). No arrest without warrant applies for parking violations alone. However, leaving a broken-down vehicle on a road lane without reflectors is a more serious offence (100–200 JOD or imprisonment 1–2 months).

Q: What happens if the parking fine is not paid within 30 days?
A: The fine is doubled and the case is referred to the competent court.

Q: Which is more serious — wrong-way driving or wrong parking?
A: Wrong-way driving is significantly more serious. It is a criminal offence carrying imprisonment of 1–3 months and fines of 250–500 JOD. Wrong parking is a minor infraction with fines of 15–40 JOD and no imprisonment.

Q: Can an officer arrest a driver for wrong parking?
A: No. Wrong parking does not trigger arrest without warrant provisions. It is a minor infraction — a ticket is issued.

Q: Can an officer arrest a driver for wrong-way driving?
A: Potentially yes — if the wrong-way driving causes injury or death, is combined with reckless driving, or the driver is under the influence. Article 25 (arrest without warrant) may apply in those cases.

Q: What are the fine ranges for each violation?
A: Wrong-way driving (divided road): 250–500 JOD. Wrong-way driving (undivided): 50–100 JOD. Wrong parking: 15–40 JOD depending on violation type.

TITLE: Impact on Driver License and Points for Both Violations
LEGAL_TEXT:
Q: Does wrong-way driving affect the driver's points record?
A: Yes. Wrong-way driving as a serious violation adds points to the driver's record under the points system. Accumulating 16 or more points in a year leads to license suspension.

Q: Is the driving license seized for wrong-way driving?
A: Yes. For wrong-way driving on a divided road, the driving license is seized pending payment of the fine. For more serious cases (injury/death), the license is suspended by court order for 6 months to 2 years.

Q: Is the driving license seized for wrong parking?
A: Only for specific parking violations — mainly parking large trucks in residential areas. For regular wrong parking violations (sidewalk, double parking, etc.), the license is generally not seized — only a fine ticket is issued.

Q: Can wrong-way driving lead to license cancellation?
A: In extreme cases (repeated violations or causing death), the court may order permanent license cancellation in addition to imprisonment and fines.

Q: How many points does wrong-way driving add?
A: The exact point value for each violation is set by regulation and can change. Officers should verify current point values through the PSD's traffic point system database.

Q: What happens if a driver accumulates 32 or more points?
A: The driving license is suspended for one full year. All points are written off when the suspension period is served.

PENALTY: License seizure for wrong-way driving. Possible suspension 6 months–2 years (court order). License seizure for large truck wrong parking in residential areas.
